Appendix: The mathematical foundation of the decision effectiveness curve

The decision effectiveness curve is grounded in exponential decay functions that model how decision effectiveness changes over time as the defender races against the attacker's momentum. Each strategy follows a distinct mathematical pattern that reflects its underlying operational reality.

Generic Formula
(Exponential Decay)
E(t) = A · e−kt
E(t)
Effectiveness at time t
A
Initial effectiveness (value at t = 0)
k
Decay rate (how fast effectiveness decreases)
t
Time to decide

Effectiveness decreases exponentially over time. A higher decay rate (k) means a faster loss of effectiveness.
